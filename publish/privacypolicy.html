Privacy Policy for QR Toolkit

Last updated: September 24, 2026

QR Toolkit ("the App") is developed to provide QR code scanning, QR code generation, and related utilities. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

In short: QR Toolkit is designed to process QR code content primarily on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

QR Toolkit does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, address, or phone number to use the core features of the App.

Depending on the features you use, the App may request access to certain device features.

2. Camera Permission

QR Toolkit may request access to your device camera to scan QR codes. Camera access is used only for QR code scanning functionality.

Camera data is processed on your device for the purpose of detecting and reading QR codes. QR Toolkit does not intentionally upload camera images or video recordings to our servers.

3. Location Permission

Certain QR code features may allow you to create or use QR codes containing location information. If you choose to use a location-based feature, the App may request access to your device's location.

Location access is used only to provide the requested feature. Location information is not collected for advertising purposes by QR Toolkit.

4. QR Code Content

QR Toolkit may process information that you choose to enter into the App, including:

  • Text
  • Website URLs
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Contact information
  • Wi-Fi information
  • Location information
  • Other QR-compatible content

QR code content is processed to provide the functionality requested by you. You are responsible for ensuring that you have permission to use any information you enter or encode into a QR code.

5. Storage of Data

QR Toolkit may store certain generated QR codes, scan history, preferences, or other app-related data locally on your device, depending on the features enabled in the App.

Locally stored information remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to share, export, or otherwise transmit it.

6. Sharing and Exporting

The App may provide features that allow you to share QR codes, generated images, or other content using applications installed on your device.

When you use a sharing feature, the information is transferred according to the privacy practices of the application or service you choose to share with.

7. Third-Party Services

QR Toolkit may use third-party services such as Google Play Services, Google AdMob, or other services required for app functionality, analytics, advertising, or distribution.

These third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies. QR Toolkit does not control the information collected directly by these third-party services.

8. Advertising

If advertising is enabled in QR Toolkit, advertisements may be provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect or use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and interaction data in accordance with their own policies and applicable laws.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices through Google's privacy policy.

9. Children's Privacy

QR Toolkit is not specifically directed toward children under the applicable minimum age for providing consent to data processing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

QR Toolkit does not maintain a central account containing your personal QR code data. Information stored locally by the App remains on your device until it is deleted by you, the App is uninstalled, or the relevant feature removes it.

12. Your Choices

You can control permissions granted to QR Toolkit through your Android device settings. You may also delete locally stored App data or uninstall the App at any time.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
